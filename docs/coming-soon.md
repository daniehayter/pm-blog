There is plenty of product management advice online. Much of it makes the work look cleaner and more predictable than it really is.

I’m creating PM Signal to share what product management looks like in practice. It will combine short videos, written guides, examples, and conversations with other people doing the work.

## What you’ll find here

I’ll cover discovery, prioritisation, roadmaps, metrics, stakeholder relationships, and the decisions that product managers make every day.

Some posts will explain a specific tool or technique. Others will examine a product decision, share an experiment, or answer a question from the community. The aim is to make each topic clear and useful.

## Who it’s for

I’m making PM Signal for product managers, aspiring PMs, founders, designers, engineers, and anyone helping a team decide what to build.

I don’t have a perfect system to sell. I want to share what I know, test new ideas, and learn from people with different experiences.

## Coming soon

I’m preparing the first set of videos and articles now. The site will grow gradually as new topics are published.

If there is a product management topic you would like to see covered, I’d like to hear about it.
